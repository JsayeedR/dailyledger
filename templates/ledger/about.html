Overview

What is DailyLedger?

DailyLedger replaces a manual Excel or Google Sheet expense tracker with a proper web app. Every registered user gets their own completely isolated financial workspace — categorized expenses and income, custom payment methods, monthly budgets with alerts, a calendar view, and a familiar spreadsheet-style monthly breakdown.

It's built for a small, trusted group of people who each want their own private ledger — not a public finance app, and not something anyone else on the team can browse into.

Trust

Security & privacy

Your data is yours alone — not even a Super Admin can see it.

  • Every transaction, category, and budget is tied to your own isolated workspace, enforced in code on every single request — not just hidden in the interface.
  • Financial data is never reachable through the admin panel, including by accounts with full admin/superuser access.
  • Passwords are hashed with industry-standard one-way hashing — never stored or visible in plain text, not even to developers.
  • All traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
  • New accounts require Super Admin approval before they can log in at all.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can anyone else see my transactions?

No. Every query in the app is scoped to your own workspace, and financial data is never exposed through the admin panel — not even to Super Admins.

Can I add my own categories and payment methods?

Yes — anytime, from Others → Manage Categories. Existing ones can be deactivated (hidden) but never deleted, so your history stays intact.

How do new accounts get approved?

Registrations start pending. A Super Admin reviews and approves (or rejects) each one before login is possible.

How do the Email/Telegram summaries work?

Choose a frequency in Notification Preferences. A Super Admin approves the request, and from then on your summary arrives automatically by Email and/or Telegram.

Can I export my data?

Reports and Detail Reports charts can be exported as JPG images directly from the page.
